# Core employment corpus prototype

The pinned dataset snapshot is `th1nhng0/vietnamese-legal-documents` revision `8977887f17be2defae4c5171d55562e1cde7d695`. The all-record audit inventories `pham_vi` before filtering: 52 raw values (51 normalized), with 56,730 exact central, 114,668 local, 51 missing, and 107 unrecognized metadata rows out of 171,556. These are audit classifications, not legal determinations.

Before article parsing or embedding, the audit checks content coverage, issuer conflicts, duplicate candidates, and relationships. It found 732 metadata records without content, 1 central-scope/local-issuer conflict, 19,578 records participating in bibliographic-identity duplicate groups, and 17,706 distinct missing relationship targets. The audit kept 34,459 rows in its mechanically clean central set; excluded 114,668 local rows and quarantined the remaining 22,271 central rows for recorded quality/conflict reasons. Issue flags overlap. See `data/audit/<revision>/pham_vi_inventory.csv`, `document_decisions.csv`, `duplicate_groups.csv`, and `summary.json` for row-level evidence and exact reason counts. Article parsing and embedding were both false for this stage.

The separate prototype corpus selects only IDs `139264`, `152668`, and `146696`, and requires exact central scope, content, and issuer checks. Its staged corpus retains 365 parsed article versions: 61 match probation, contracts, working time, or leave; 7 match the explicit dependency allowlist; and 297 are excluded from retrieval as out of scope. Other articles stay in the curated file with `retrieval_index_candidate=false`. Core article staging reuses active parse records for IDs `152668` and `146696`; it parses `139264` once because the active build had quarantined that bibliographic duplicate. Curation consumes the checksummed stage artifact and does not parse source HTML.

The Labor Code English translation `vbpqta_11135` is suppressed in favor of the Vietnamese original by the pinned bibliographic identity ledger; text is not merged. Amendment, partial-effect, replacement, repeal, and consolidation relationships touching the three core documents are retained in the corpus manifest. Related noncore texts are quarantined, so the corpus is explicitly a base-snapshot corpus, not a consolidated text. Such questions abstain. All records have `current_validity=unverified`; blank expiry is unknown. The crawl is not proof of current legal validity or official-source verification.

The corpus manifest remains inactive and every record has `answer_evidence_enabled=false`. Employment scope remains probation, contracts, working time, and leave. The opt-in app reads the digest-versioned local Qdrant collection and uses BM25+dense RRF. It produces an exact source extract only for a clearly phrased source-text request. Applicability, ambiguous permission, as-of-date, current-validity, and amendment/consolidation questions abstain. No generated legal conclusion is supported.
